What is a subscript?

A subscript sits below the baseline, like the 2 in H₂O. Unicode gives you actual characters (U+2080 to U+209C for many digits), not a smaller font style that disappears when you copy text between apps.

  • Chemical formulas (H₂O, CO₂, C₆H₁₂O₆)
  • Mathematical notation (x₁, aₙ, variables)
  • Scientific notation and equations
  • Footnote references and indices
  • Molecular formulas in documents
