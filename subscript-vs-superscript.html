What is the opposite of subscript?

Quick answer: The opposite of subscript is superscript. Subscript sits below the baseline (H₂O). Superscript sits above it (x²). Mix them up in a lab report and the meaning changes. Chemistry wants atom counts below the line. Math wants powers and footnote marks above it.

When do you use each style in science?

Chemistry and physics lean on lowered digits for counts and indices. Math and units lean on raised digits for powers.

  • Water and carbon dioxide: H₂O, CO₂ (atom counts below the line)
  • Squared and cubed: x², 10³, m² (exponents and area units above the line)
  • Initial values: v₀ (index below), not v⁰
  • Ions: Na⁺ uses a raised charge, not a lowered one

Rule of thumb: atom counts and indices go below; powers, charges, and footnote markers go above.
