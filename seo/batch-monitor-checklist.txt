Post-deploy monitoring (14-21 days per batch)

Metrics (GSC):
- Primary query: impressions, clicks, CTR, position
- Cannibalization: secondary URL impressions on owner query
- Fan-out H2 query impressions
- Referring domains (Links report)

Rollback trigger (ALL must hold before revert):
1. Position drop >5 places on a protecting URL
2. Click OR impression decline sustained across 2 consecutive 7-day windows
3. Query has at least ~10 impressions/week (below = noise, do not revert)

Batch 1 watch queries: subscript generator, subscript copy paste, subscript text generator
Batch 1 deploy date: record when homepage ships
Next batch earliest: 14 days after Batch 1 deploy if no rollback
