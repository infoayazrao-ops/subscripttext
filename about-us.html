Our Goal

subscripttext.com is a free subscript generator that helps you convert plain text into real Unicode subscript characters. Our goal is to make subscript text easy to create for chemistry formulas (like H₂O and CO₂), math notation, and academic or technical writing.

We focus on:

  • Making subscript and superscript conversion simple, with no HTML or keyboard shortcuts needed
  • Output that works everywhere: Word, Google Docs, social media, and email
  • Supporting real-world use: chemistry, math, physics, and documents

The tool also includes superscript (for exponents like x²) and small text as extra options, all in one place.

What We Offer

Our subscript generator gives you:

  • Formula mode | Turn H2O into H₂O, CO2 into CO₂, and C6H12O6 into C₆H₁₂O₆ with one click
  • Subscript for chemistry and math | Ideal for chemical formulas and equations

  • Browser-based | no signup, no install, works offline after first load
