What is superscript?

Superscript is text above the baseline, think x², E=mc², or a footnote marker ¹. It is not the same as making font size smaller; Unicode superscript characters stay when you copy between Word, Google Docs, and chat apps.

Quick answer: Use superscript for powers, footnotes, and raised symbols. Open our generator, choose Superscript, type your text, and copy the result.

When do you need superscript?

  • Math powers: x², y³, aⁿ
  • Science: E=mc², 10⁶
  • Footnotes: markers ¹ ² ³

What is the opposite of superscript?

Subscript sits below the line (H₂O, x₁). If you need both on one page, switch modes on the generator or read subscript vs superscript.
