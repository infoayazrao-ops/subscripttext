How do you copy and paste superscript text?

Quick answer: Open our tool, select Superscript, type x2 or 1, click Copy, and paste.

Footnotes (¹) and exponents (x²) are the usual cases. See what is superscript for a short explainer.

How to Use Superscript Copy Paste

  1. Open the online generator.
  2. Select Superscript as the conversion type.
  3. Type or paste text (examples: x2, E=mc2, 1 for a footnote marker).
  4. Use Formula mode + Numbers Only: On if only digits should become superscript.
  5. Click Copy and paste into Word, Google Docs, WhatsApp, PowerPoint, or email.

Common Uses

  • Exponents: x², y³, 10⁴
  • Footnotes: Many people search for footnote copy and paste or 1 footnote copy and paste, use superscript ¹ ² ³ from the generator.
  • Equations: E=mc² style text (paste as Unicode, not LaTeX).
  • Superscript in WhatsApp: Same as subscript, WhatsApp shows Unicode superscript correctly. See also subscript in WhatsApp.

Superscript vs Subscript

Superscript sits above the line (powers, footnotes). Subscript sits below (chemistry indices). Read subscript vs superscript for a simple comparison.
