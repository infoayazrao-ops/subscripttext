Need a raised 2 for x² or m²?

Quick answer: Need superscript 2? Tap ² below. It copies as Unicode (U+00B2), so it stays raised in chat.

Published 2 May 2026 · Updated 24 August 2026

Tap to copy

Then paste. Word’s Format → Superscript will not survive WhatsApp; these characters will.

The Word trap

Ctrl + Shift + = in Word makes a 2 look raised. Paste that sentence into a message and the 2 drops back. The app never received a raised character — only a font switch.

² is a different code point. Once it is on the clipboard, WhatsApp, Docs, and most phones keep it up. Footnotes use the same set: ¹ ² ³, not lowered ₁.

Units and squares people copy most: x², m², cm³, E=mc². Tap those chips if you only need one token. For 10² or a custom base, type the plain text in the converter with Formula on so only the digits rise.

On a phone: tap ², switch to the message or Notes, long-press, Paste. You should see a 2 sitting above the line. On a computer: click ², then Ctrl + V or Cmd + V. If you still see a full-size 2, you copied a normal digit — tap the chip again, not a 2 you typed yourself.

Water and CO₂ use the lowered set. Do not mix ₂ into an exponent. If a classmate sees a box, their font pack is missing ² — they still received the right character.
