Why Word’s lowered 2 dies in chat

In Word or Google Docs you can highlight a 2 and pick Format → Subscript. It looks right on that page. Paste the same sentence into WhatsApp and the 2 jumps back to normal size. That style never left the document.

₂ is a different character. Once it is on the clipboard, most phones and browsers keep it below the line. If a very old font shows a box, the device is missing U+2080–U+2089 — switch to a common system font, not a smaller point size.

On a phone: tap ₂, switch to the chat or Notes, long-press, Paste. You should see a 2 sitting under the line, the same shape as the 2 in H₂O. If you still see a full-size 2, you copied a normal digit by mistake — tap the large ₂ button again, not a 2 you typed yourself.

On a computer: click ₂, then Ctrl + V (Windows) or Cmd + V (Mac). The same glyph works in Gmail and most slide text boxes. Spreadsheet formulas still want H2O or CO2 as plain ASCII if you are calculating; use ₂ in labels and titles.
